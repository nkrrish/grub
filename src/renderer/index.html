    Behind the scenes
    This is the engine Grub drives for you. You can watch, or type if you like.

        Hi, I'm Grub.

        I eat the junk your Mac piles up: caches, leftovers, forgotten installers. Let's get me set up. It takes about a minute.

        First, my teeth.

        Grub does its chewing with a small, free cleaning engine. Let's make sure it's on your Mac.

        Checking…

        Let me into every room.

        Some of the biggest messes hide in places macOS keeps locked. Full Disk Access lets Grub look there. Grub only reads and removes what you approve.

        1. Click Open Settings.
        2. Turn on Grub in the list. Add it with + if it's missing.
        3. Come back here. This screen updates on its own.
        Not allowed yet

        Let me peek at login items.

        To show which apps open when you log in, and switch them off for you, Grub needs to talk to System Events. macOS will ask once.

        A couple of preferences. You can change them any time from the version number at the bottom of the sidebar.

        Open Grub at loginKeeps Grub in the menu bar with live stats.
        Keep the engine updatedChecks once a day and updates quietly.

        Some optimizations need your Mac password. Grub asks for it in its own window, only when needed, and never stores it.

        Grub

        A free, open-source Mac cleaner: health at a glance, a disk map, guided cleanups, uninstalls, startup items and updates. Its cleaning runs on Mole, the open-source engine by tw93 (GPL-3.0). Grub isn't affiliated with Mole. For Mole's official app, see mole.fit.
