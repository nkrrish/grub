# Code of Conduct

Grub follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome in issues, discussions, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives.

Report problems privately to the maintainer at grub@attechy.com. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer may remove content or block anyone who breaks these rules.
